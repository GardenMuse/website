Instructions for use

  1. 1

    For potted plants

    Mix 1 to 2 handfuls (10 to 20% of total soil) into the top layer, or blend 1 part compost with 4 parts soil for new pots.

  2. 2

    For garden beds

    Spread a 1-inch layer around the plant base, or gently mix it into the top 2 to 3 inches of soil.

  3. 3

    For lawns

    Sprinkle evenly over the grass and water lightly right away.

  4. 4

    Quick tip

    Apply every 2 to 4 weeks during the growing season and always water after applying.
