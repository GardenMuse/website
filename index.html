Nothing we make
is invented.

A coconut husk becomes a growing medium. Earthworms turn waste into plant food. Neem has guarded Indian crops for centuries. We synthesise none of it. We source it, clean it, pack it, and leave the rest to nature.

Read our roots

Born to care for nature, naturally.

We have never been beach or mountain people. We aren't cat or dog people, either. We are garden people, and everything we pack starts with what we would put in our own soil.

  • Organic, or we don't make it

    No synthetic additives in anything we pack. If we would not put it in our own pots, it does not carry our name.

  • Grown, not manufactured

    Coir, castings and neem. Every ingredient was alive before it reached you. None of it was invented in a lab.

  • Right for pots and plots

    Balcony containers, raised beds, lawns and hydroponics. Sized, rinsed and explained for each of them.

  • Packed by garden people

    Made and packed by S&M CoirCo in Perumbavoor, Kerala. Family run since long before it was a business.
